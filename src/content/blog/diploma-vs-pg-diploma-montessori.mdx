IMTTI Kazhakkoottam offers three Montessori teaching practice courses. Choosing the right one depends on your educational background, career goal, and how much time you have. Here is a direct breakdown.

## Who Should Choose the PG Diploma – 1 Year?

The **International PG Diploma in Montessori Teaching Practice (1 year)** covers the full scope: 8 theory papers, all 40 Montessori wooden apparatus with unlimited practice hours, and 5 chart & album works.

Choose the 1-year PG Diploma if:

- You hold a Plus Two, Degree, or Masters and want the deepest Montessori grounding.
- You are preparing for a senior Montessori role, a school leadership position, or plan to start your own preschool.
- You are targeting Gulf employment and want maximum preparation time before applying.
- You prefer a steady pace rather than an intensive schedule.

Both PG Diploma programmes carry **UK CACHE Level 3 certification** in addition to IAO-USA – the standard Gulf employers ask for.

## Who Should Choose the PG Diploma – 6-Month Crash Course?

The **6-month crash course** delivers the same PG Diploma credential on the same syllabus (8 theory papers, 40 apparatus) but compressed, with 2 chart & album works instead of 5.

Choose the crash course if:

- You need to qualify quickly before a job posting or Gulf visa application.
- You are a Degree holder comfortable with an intensive pace.
- You want the full PG Diploma title without the year-long commitment.

The credential and certifications (IAO-USA + CACHE Level 3 + embassy attestation eligibility) are identical to the 1-year programme.

## Who Should Choose the Diploma – 6 Months?

The **International Diploma in Montessori Teaching Practice (6 months)** is the entry-level qualification, open from **10th pass** (SSLC). It covers 6 theory papers and 20 apparatus.

Choose the Diploma if:

- Your highest qualification is 10th or 12th standard.
- You are stepping into early childhood education for the first time.
- You are a homemaker looking for a part-time professional qualification.
- You want to teach at a local Montessori preschool near Kazhakkoottam or Technopark.

The Diploma carries IAO-USA certification and embassy attestation eligibility. It does not include CACHE Level 3 (which is a PG-level certification).

## What About Gulf Jobs?

For Gulf employment (UAE, Qatar, Kuwait, Bahrain), most Montessori schools require a **recognised teacher-training qualification with embassy attestation**. The UK CACHE Level 3 is specifically recognised by Gulf school chains and licensing bodies. This means the **PG Diploma** (either version) is the stronger choice for Gulf applicants.

The Diploma is a valid credential but may not meet the minimum threshold for Gulf visa sponsorship at some schools. If Gulf employment is your primary goal, choose a PG Diploma course.
