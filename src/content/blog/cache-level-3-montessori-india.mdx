If you have been researching Montessori jobs in the Gulf — UAE, Qatar, Kuwait, Bahrain — you have likely come across the requirement for **CACHE Level 3**. This guide explains exactly what it is, why Gulf employers ask for it, and how Indian teachers can earn it.

## What is CACHE Level 3?

**CACHE** stands for the **Council for Awards in Care, Health and Education** — a UK awarding body specialising in early years, childcare, and education qualifications. The **Level 3** qualification maps to the UK Regulated Qualifications Framework (RQF) Level 3, which is roughly equivalent to an A-level in terms of academic level but focused on practical early childhood competencies.

CACHE Level 3 programmes typically cover:

- Child development (birth to 5 years)
- Observation and assessment of young children
- Healthy child development and safeguarding
- Early years curriculum approaches (including Montessori)
- Inclusive practice and working with families

## Why Do Gulf Employers Ask for CACHE Level 3?

The Gulf's private school licensing authorities — such as KHDA (Knowledge and Human Development Authority) in Dubai — maintain lists of recognised qualifications. CACHE Level 3 appears on these lists because it is:

1. **British-originated** – The Gulf's private school sector has historically been modelled on the British system, so UK qualifications carry weight.
2. **Internationally portable** – It is recognised not just in the Gulf but also in the UK, Canada, Australia, and other countries.
3. **Practical and standards-based** – It certifies applied classroom competency, not just theoretical knowledge.

Many Gulf Montessori schools and nurseries will not shortlist applicants without at least a CACHE Level 3 or equivalent.

## What is Embassy Attestation and Why Do You Need It?

For Gulf visa applications, certificates must be **attested** — a process where:

1. The certificate is verified by the issuing institution
2. Attested by the State Government (HRD / Education Department)
3. Verified by the Ministry of External Affairs (MEA), India
4. Finally attested by the concerned country's consulate (UAE, Qatar, etc.)

IMTTI provides **embassy attestation support** for Gulf-bound students — guiding you through the MEA and consulate steps. Without attestation, your certificate may not be accepted for Gulf visa or employment purposes.
